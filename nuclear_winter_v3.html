Tier 2 food-system effects

Food impacts are shown as production-side stress indicators, not direct predictions of access, trade, or mortality. Mortality outputs have been removed in this version because they created false precision downstream of poorly modelled institutional effects.

What's worth flagging as implausible?

  • If lofting efficiency is low but lifetime is very high, you're probably creating an internally inconsistent branch.
  • If food sensitivity is high, remember that this stands in for crop, trade, storage, substitution, and adaptation all at once — it is not a crop model.
  • Very specific-looking outputs from the intermediate scenario remain less defensible than anchor cases.
  • This model still says more about conditional pathways than about forecasts.
